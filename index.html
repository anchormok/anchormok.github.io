About

About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Bone Marrow Transplantation Center of the First Affiliated Hospital, Zhejiang University School of Medicine, and the Liangzhu Laboratory, where I work at the interface of cellular immunotherapy and cancer genomics.

My work focuses on engineering chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T and NK cells for hematologic malignancies, integrating allogeneic transplantation with CAR-based bridging therapy, and applying single-cell and spatial multi-omics to dissect the tumor immune microenvironment.

My research has been published in the New England Journal of Medicine and Cell Reports Medicine, covering next-generation CAR-T strategies and iPSC-derived "off-the-shelf" CAR-NK cells.

Research

Research Interests

🧫

Cellular Immunotherapy

Engineering next-generation CAR-T and CAR-NK cells for hematologic malignancies, including iPSC-derived universal cell products.

🩸

Hematologic Malignancies

Acute myeloid leukemia, T-cell acute lymphoblastic leukemia/lymphoma, and B-cell lymphoma — biology and novel therapeutic strategies.

🔬

Single-Cell & Spatial Omics

Decoding the tumor immune microenvironment at single-cell and spatial resolution to guide immunotherapy design.

🧠

Tumor Immunology

Mechanisms of immune evasion, myeloid cell biology, and the immunosuppressive microenvironment of blood cancers.

🤖

Computational Biology & ML

Biomarker discovery and prognostic modeling for precision medicine using multi-omics data and machine learning.

🛡️

Transplant Immunology

Optimizing the integration of CAR-based therapy with allogeneic hematopoietic stem-cell transplantation.

News & Updates

2026

CD19 CAR-T long-term follow-up published

Our long-term follow-up study of CD19 CAR-T in relapsed/refractory B-cell NHL — identifying Ki-67 as a prognostic factor for sustained remission — is out in Transplantation and Cellular Therapy.

2025

iPSC-derived CAR-NK cells in Cell Reports Medicine

Our study on CD70-targeted iPSC-derived CAR-NK cells — potent against tumors and alloreactive T cells — was published in Cell Reports Medicine.

Landmark study in the New England Journal of Medicine

Sequential CD7 CAR T-cell therapy followed by allogeneic HSCT without GVHD prophylaxis — a new paradigm for T-ALL/LBL — published in NEJM.
